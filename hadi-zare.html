My Research

Developing Strategies for Wind Turbine Control to Enhance Energy Efficiency

AI-Driven Control Strategies with Digital Twin Integration

This project develops AI-driven control strategies for wind turbines, integrating digital twin technology for real-time adaptive optimization. By modeling turbine dynamics and analyzing operational data, we aim to maximize power output, extend equipment lifespan, and enhance system reliability under variable wind conditions.

AI Control • Digital Twin • Real-Time Optimization • Adaptive Systems

My Projects

Integrating artificial intelligence and digital twin technology to enable intelligent, adaptive control systems for next-generation wind turbines.

AI-Based Adaptive Control Systems

Developing advanced control strategies using machine learning algorithms to analyze operational data and predict wind speed changes. Enables real-time adaptive adjustment of blade pitch and yaw to maximize power output while reducing mechanical loads under variable wind conditions.

Machine Learning • Pitch Control • Power Optimization

High-Fidelity Digital Twin Modeling

Establishing high-fidelity digital twin models that accurately simulate turbine dynamics through virtual simulation. Combined with IoT sensor data, enables real-time equipment health monitoring, fault prediction, and extends the lifespan of critical components like main bearings and gearboxes.

Virtual Simulation • IoT Sensors • Predictive Maintenance
