Advanced Manufacturing and Numerical Damage Analysis of the Driving Rotor in Vertical-Axis Wind Turbines

Advanced Manufacturing and Numerical Damage Analysis of the Driving Rotor in Vertical-Axis Wind Turbines

This project aims to develop a lightweight yet durable blade design for vertical-axis wind turbines via numerical damage analysis, with detailed consideration of real-world boundary conditions and loading scenarios. To implement this design, the project aims to propose an advanced manufacturing approach that minimises material waste and manufacturing costs.

FEM Analysis • Damage Mechanics • Advanced Manufacturing • Cost Optimization

My Projects

Combining expertise in CAD/CAM, finite element analysis, and continuum damage mechanics to design next-generation VAWT blades that balance structural integrity with material efficiency, ensuring optimal performance under real-world operating conditions.

Lightweight Vertical-Axis Turbine (VAWT) Blade Design

Developing optimized blade geometries through numerical simulation and damage analysis, focusing on fatigue resistance, structural durability, and manufacturing feasibility. The research integrates FEM-based stress analysis with advanced material characterization to achieve high-performance, cost-effective blade designs.

CAD/CAM • FEM Simulation • Fatigue Analysis • Material Optimization
