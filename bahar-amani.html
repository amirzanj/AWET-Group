My Project

Wind-Integrated Hybrid Energy Systems for Off-Grid Applications

Exergy-Based Design and Analysis of Decentralized VAWT-Integrated Hybrid Energy Systems

Conducting an exergoeconomic study on the feasibility of employing small-scale VAWTs in hybrid energy systems for off-grid applications in Australia. This research explores the integration of vertical-axis wind turbines with other renewable energy sources to develop sustainable, cost-effective solutions for remote and decentralized power generation.
